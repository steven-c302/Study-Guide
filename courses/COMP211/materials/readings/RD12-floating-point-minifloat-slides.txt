[Slide 3]
Bias
• Why do we use a bias instead of 2’s complement?
• Simplifies Comparison Operations
• With a biased exponent, larger numbers have higher bit values, and smaller numbers
have lower bit values, making it easy to compare them as unsigned integers.
• If two’s complement were used, negative exponents (which have leading 1s) would be
greater in unsigned comparison, breaking the ordering.
